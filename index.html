Good to know

Tie-breakers

  • Walmart, Target, warehouse clubs don't count as supermarkets for Amex 6%.
  • Restaurant inside a hotel or store, or a bakery may not code as a restaurant for Discover.
  • Pay utilities online or by phone, directly to the provider — not at a carrier store or through a landlord/HOA.
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay / PayPal can hide the merchant category. For bonus categories, use the physical card or pay directly when in doubt.
  • Discover's $1,500 cap is shared across all its 5% categories each quarter. Big utility bills use it up fast — check progress in the app.
  • Amex $6,000 grocery cap is per calendar year (resets Jan 1). After that, groceries earn 1%.

Travel

  • Rental cars: always Chase (primary coverage). Decline the rental company's CDW.
  • Abroad: Chase first, Discover as backup (less accepted overseas), leave Amex home (2.7% fee).
  • Pay the whole trip with Chase to get trip cancellation, delay and baggage coverage.

Points & fees

  • Chase points: best used for travel (airline transfers or Points Boost in Chase Travel). Cashing out at 1¢ wastes value.
  • Chase 2026 changes: 10% anniversary bonus discontinued (last payout by Jan 31, 2027). Hyatt transfers now 4:3. Most other partners still 1:1.
  • Chase hotel credit: the first $100 of Chase Travel hotel spend earns no points; portal bookings often skip hotel loyalty points.
  • Both $95 fees can pay for themselves: Amex via the Disney credit + groceries; Chase via the hotel credit + DoorDash promos.
  • Pay in full every month. Set autopay for the statement balance on all three — interest wipes out rewards.
  • Keep Discover open — no fee, and it helps credit age and utilization.
